What gets improved

eBay listings usually need more than a new title.

The strongest gains often come from aligning the full listing: search terms, titles, item specifics, photos, descriptions, policies, and buyer-trust cues.

Search and title structure

  • product type and buyer search terms
  • fit, size, material, quantity, and condition language
  • clearer title order without filler

Item specifics and product facts

  • category fields shoppers filter by
  • model, part, size, color, and compatibility details
  • cleaner condition, contents, and policy cues

Photos and page trust

  • gallery order and detail shots
  • packaging, included pieces, and scale cues
  • description and account gaps that slow decisions
