FAQ

Common questions before starting.

Do I need to give account access?

No. For the audit, listing or store links are enough to begin. If deeper access is ever needed later, we would discuss that separately.

Do you rewrite the listings inside the audit?

No. The audit identifies the priority fixes and next steps. Done-for-you rewrites or listing cleanup are separate paid work.

Is sales growth guaranteed?

No. The audit improves clarity and identifies marketplace issues, but sales also depend on demand, pricing, competition, traffic, inventory, and other factors.

What happens after the audit?

You can stop there, request the 5-day Listing Optimization Sprint, or ask about managed Amazon and eBay optimization if your account needs ongoing support.

Which audit should I choose?
