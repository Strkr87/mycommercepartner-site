What does MyCommercePartner do?

We provide hands-on Amazon and eBay account-management support for product brands: listing cleanup, marketplace content, account-health review, and weekly progress reporting.

Do you only work with Amazon and eBay?

Amazon and eBay are the main focus for the homepage offer. We can also discuss related product-page or website issues if they affect how buyers evaluate the brand.

What is the free listing audit?

It is a focused first look at a small sample of your marketplace presence. We flag the clearest listing, content, or account-priority issues before deeper work.

What happens after the audit?

If the opportunity is clear, we can point you toward a Listing Optimization Sprint or ongoing managed marketplace support. If a smaller fix is enough, we will say that.

What is Listing Rescue?

Listing Rescue is the focused cleanup path for stale or underperforming listings, especially eBay listings that need stronger titles, clearer descriptions, and better buyer confidence.

Can you manage ongoing marketplace tasks?

Yes. Managed support is for brands that want listing updates, content improvements, account review, and weekly marketplace priorities handled more consistently.

Will I still approve important changes?

Yes. The goal is to reduce your workload, not remove your judgment. Our team can prepare and organize the work so approvals are easier.

Do you still offer Website Reviews?

Yes, but Website Reviews are now secondary to the main marketplace account-management offer. They are still available for brands whose website is hurting buyer trust or inquiries.

What about Missed Lead Recovery?

That is a separate local-business service. It remains available, but the main MyCommercePartner homepage is focused on product brands selling through Amazon and eBay.

How do I contact you?

Use the contact form instead of opening an email app. Send your name, email, store or listing link, and what you want improved first.
