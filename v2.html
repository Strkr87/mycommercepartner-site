FAQ

Questions sellers ask.

Do you need access to my eBay account?

No. The optimizer reads the public listing page, plus anything you type in. You paste the results into eBay yourself.

Will the AI make things up about my item?

No. It's instructed to use only facts from your listing and the details you add. When something important is missing, like battery health or included accessories, it shows up in your fix list instead of in the copy.

What counts as a credit?

One optimization of one listing. Re-running the same listing after adding details uses another credit.

Does it work for Amazon?

eBay comes first. Amazon-style titles are supported when you set the marketplace, but the product is built around eBay.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are month to month through Stripe, and you can manage or cancel from your dashboard.
