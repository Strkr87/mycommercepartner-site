## Why start here

A Marketplace Listing Audit gives the seller a low-risk first step. MyCommercePartner reviews the listings that matter most and identifies the clearest fixes before quoting a larger package.

This keeps the process simple:

1. Seller sends Amazon/eBay listing links.
2. MyCommercePartner reviews the priority issues.
3. Seller receives plain-English recommendations.
4. If there is enough upside, seller can move into Store Cleanup, Monthly Marketplace Management, or a 60-day trial.
