## 1. One-Sentence Positioning

MyCommercePartner helps product brands and online sellers clean up, manage, and grow their Amazon and eBay marketplace presence without hiring a full-time marketplace team.

## 2. Offer Ladder

### Offer 1 — Marketplace Listing Audit

**Role in ladder:** Low-friction entry offer that identifies the highest-impact problems and creates a clear path into paid cleanup or management.

**Best for:** Sellers who know their listings/store could perform better but do not know what to fix first.

**Scope:**
- Review up to 10 Amazon or eBay listings, or one small storefront/category snapshot.
- Assess titles, descriptions, bullets/item specifics, images, pricing signals, shipping/trust cues, category fit, and buyer confidence issues.
- Provide a prioritized findings report: what is hurting visibility, conversion, trust, and completeness.
- Include top 3 quick wins and recommended next package.
- Does not include implementation, account support, customer support, advertising management, or inventory work.

**Pricing:**
- Intro audit: $97 for up to 5 listings.
- Standard audit: $197 for up to 10 listings.
- Larger audit: quote only if there is a clear scope; default to converting them into Cleanup or Monthly Management instead of custom analysis.

**Outcome:**
- Client receives a practical action list showing where their marketplace presence is leaking sales or trust.
- MyCommercePartner gets a qualified diagnostic view before proposing deeper work.

**Delivery time:**
- 2-3 business days after intake/access/screenshots are received.

**Upsell path:**
- If issues are mostly listing quality: Store Cleanup Package.
- If issues are ongoing and operational: Monthly Marketplace Management.
- If seller is small but has upside and low risk: Performance-Based Trial.

### Offer 2 — Store Cleanup Package

**Role in ladder:** Fixed-scope implementation offer that turns audit findings into visible improvements without creating an open-ended service relationship.

**Best for:** Sellers with messy listings, weak titles, missing item specifics, poor descriptions, inconsistent trust signals, or outdated store presentation.

**Scope options:**

#### Basic Cleanup — $297
- Up to 5 listings.
- Improve titles, key item specifics/bullets, description structure, basic trust cues, and simple buyer-facing clarity.
- One summary of changes and next steps.

#### Standard Cleanup — $497
- Up to 10 listings.
- Everything in Basic.
- Add category/storefront review, stronger conversion notes, and a short 30-day improvement plan.

#### Store Rescue — $997
- Up to 25 listings or one focused product category.
- Everything in Standard.
- Add deeper listing standardization, priority issue map, and monthly management recommendation.

**Out of scope:**
- Daily account management.
- Buyer messages/customer service.
- Inventory management.
- PPC/ad spend management.
- Full catalog rebuilds without a new scope.
- Product photography, graphic design, compliance/legal claims, or marketplace appeals.

**Outcome:**
- Client gets cleaner listings/store presence with better buyer clarity, trust, and discoverability foundations.

**Delivery time:**
- Basic: 3-5 business days.
- Standard: 5-7 business days.
- Store Rescue: 7-10 business days.

**Upsell path:**
- Monthly Marketplace Management for continued listing improvement, reporting, seasonal optimization, and seller support.
- Performance Trial only if the client is small, has measurable sales baseline, and agrees to simple terms.

### Offer 3 — Monthly Marketplace Management

**Role in ladder:** Core recurring-revenue service. This is the main business model because it is delegatable, systemized, and not dependent on Ken personally doing every task.

**Best for:** Brands/sellers with existing sales or listings who want consistent marketplace improvement but do not want to hire a full-time Amazon/eBay specialist.

**Scope by tier:**

#### Starter Management — $500/month
- Up to 10 active listings monitored/improved per month.
- Monthly listing improvements and cleanup tasks.
- Basic performance review.
- One monthly summary report.
- Email-style async communication only; no daily support.

#### Growth Management — $1,000/month
- Up to 30 active listings monitored/improved per month.
- Ongoing title, content, item specifics, storefront/category improvement.
- Monthly performance review and recommendations.
- Marketplace task coordination checklist.
- One monthly strategy/report call or recorded summary.

#### Managed Marketplace — $2,000+/month
- Larger catalog or multi-marketplace support.
- Prioritized monthly roadmap.
- Deeper store/account cleanup and listing operations.
- Coordination with client-side team or contractors.
- Optional performance bonus on sales growth above an agreed baseline.

**Default boundaries:**
- Support is async and scheduled, not same-day customer service.
- Client keeps ownership of inventory, fulfillment, buyer messages, refunds, legal/compliance decisions, and marketplace account risk decisions unless separately scoped.
- Any emergency, appeal, suspension, or high-risk account issue requires a separate review and Ken approval before acceptance.

**Outcome:**
- Client gets an ongoing marketplace improvement team without hiring in-house.
- MyCommercePartner builds recurring revenue from scoped, repeatable work.

**Delivery time:**
- Onboarding: 5 business days after payment/access.
- First improvement cycle: within first 10 business days.
- Reporting cadence: monthly.

**Upsell path:**
- Move Starter to Growth when listing count, sales volume, or improvement volume increases.
- Add performance bonus only after baseline data is clear.
- Add Store Rescue project if the account has a backlog too large for monthly capacity.

### Offer 4 — Performance-Based Trial For Small Sellers

**Role in ladder:** Lower upfront commitment option for small sellers like NCSNA where there is upside but limited budget.

**Best for:** Small sellers with real products, some existing marketplace activity, clean business intent, and willingness to share baseline sales data.

**Default offer:**
- $200/month + 15% of monthly sales above a $1,500 baseline.
- 60-day trial.
- No long-term contract.
- Scope limited to agreed listings/store cleanup and monthly improvement actions.

**Fallback offer if 15% is resisted:**
- $250/month + 10% of monthly sales above a $1,500 baseline.
- 60-day trial.

**Simple flat option:**
- $300/month flat for 60 days.
- No performance share.
- Limited scope and no guarantee.

**Scope:**
- Audit the current store/listings.
- Improve a limited number of priority listings.
- Clean up buyer-facing trust and clarity issues.
- Review performance monthly against baseline.
- Recommend whether to continue, upgrade, or stop after 60 days.

**Outcome:**
- Seller gets help without a large upfront management fee.
- MyCommercePartner tests upside while protecting against unlimited free work.

**Delivery time:**
- Trial setup: 3-5 business days after payment, baseline, and access.
- First cleanup actions: within 7 business days.
- Trial review: day 30 and day 60.

**Upsell path:**
- Convert to Starter Management at $500/month if the trial shows traction.
- Convert to Growth Management if listing volume or revenue justifies it.
- End trial politely if seller is unresponsive, account quality is poor, or economics do not work.

## 3. Qualification Rules

### Good-Fit Clients

A prospect is a good fit when most of these are true:
- Sells physical products on Amazon, eBay, or both.
- Already has listings, inventory, or a store presence to improve.
- Has visible listing quality problems: weak titles, missing details, poor descriptions, unclear images, weak trust cues, inconsistent store setup.
- Wants practical marketplace support, not vague consulting.
- Can provide access, screenshots, listing URLs, and baseline sales data when needed.
- Accepts scoped packages and defined communication windows.
- Understands marketplace growth is improvement-based, not guaranteed overnight results.
- Has enough margin to support cleanup/management fees.
- Is willing to make client-side decisions on inventory, fulfillment, returns, pricing approvals, and account risk.

### Best Early Targets

Prioritize:
- Small-to-mid product brands with neglected Amazon/eBay presence.
- eBay sellers with messy stores and decent inventory.
- Amazon/eBay sellers doing some sales but lacking optimization discipline.
- Local/regional businesses with products online but no marketplace operator.
- Warm relationship/referral prospects where trust already exists.
- Sellers willing to pay for an audit before requesting custom work.

### Bad-Fit Clients

Reject or defer prospects when any of these are true:
- They expect Ken or the team to handle daily customer support, buyer messages, returns, or fulfillment.
- They want unpaid speculative work before buying an audit or trial.
- They demand guaranteed sales outcomes without baseline data or control over key variables.
- They have no real inventory, no clear product, or no marketplace account.
- They are in high-risk, counterfeit, restricted, gray-market, or compliance-heavy categories without clear documentation.
- Their account is suspended, in legal trouble, or facing a marketplace appeal unless a separate risk review is approved.
- They want unlimited custom work, constant calls, or same-day support inside a low-cost package.
- Margins are too thin to pay for delegated marketplace work.
- They resist scope boundaries or refuse to share basic performance data.

### Qualification Gate Before Accepting Paid Work

Before accepting anything beyond a basic audit, confirm:
1. Marketplace: Amazon, eBay, or both.
2. Product/category: what they sell and whether it is restricted/risky.
3. Listing count: how many listings need help.
4. Current monthly sales baseline, if available.
5. Primary pain: visibility, conversion, store cleanup, account organization, or growth support.
6. Access needed: listing URLs, seller screenshots, or account permissions.
7. Decision-maker: who approves changes and pricing.
8. Scope fit: audit, cleanup, monthly management, or performance trial.

## 4. NCSNA-Style Small-Seller Performance Trial Script

### Short Version

"Based on where your store is now, I would not recommend jumping straight into a big monthly management contract. A cleaner first step is a 60-day marketplace improvement trial. We set a simple baseline, clean up the priority listings, improve the store presentation, and measure whether the changes create enough lift to justify continuing.

The trial would be $200/month plus 15% of monthly sales above $1,500. So if the store does not grow past that baseline, your cost stays low. If it does grow, we share in the upside. After 60 days, we either move into a simple monthly plan, adjust the scope, or stop with no long-term contract."

### If They Push Back On Percentage

"Totally fair. If 15% feels too high, the alternate structure is $250/month plus 10% of monthly sales above the $1,500 baseline. That keeps the upfront cost reasonable while still making sure the work is tied to results."

### If They Want No Performance Share

"We can also keep it very simple: $300/month flat for 60 days. That would cover a limited cleanup and improvement cycle, but it would not include a performance-based upside structure or expanded support. At the end of 60 days, we decide whether the account justifies ongoing management."

### Boundary Language

"To keep the trial affordable, this would not include daily customer service, fulfillment, inventory management, marketplace appeals, or unlimited edits. The focus is marketplace presentation: listings, store cleanup, buyer trust, and priority improvements."

### Close

"If that works, the next step is simple: we confirm the current sales baseline, pick the priority listings, collect the needed access/screenshots, and start the first cleanup cycle."

## 5. No-Cold-Call Revenue Acquisition Plan

**Important rule:** The current cold outreach and follow-up outreach freeze remains active. Do not send cold emails, cold DMs, follow-ups, apology emails, or prospecting sequences unless Ken explicitly approves a compliant route. Acquisition should be inbound, warm, referral-led, partner-led, or buyer-initiated through paid audits.

### A. Inbound Website Funnel

Build the site around one clear path:
1. Visitor lands on Amazon/eBay marketplace management positioning.
2. Visitor chooses one of four offers: Audit, Cleanup, Monthly Management, Performance Trial.
3. Primary CTA: "Request a Marketplace Review" or "Book a Paid Listing Audit."
4. Intake form qualifies marketplace, product category, listing count, sales baseline, and primary pain.
5. Default conversion: paid audit unless the prospect is already clearly qualified for management.

Key website sections:
- Clear positioning headline.
- Four offer cards with price anchors.
- Who this is for / not for.
- Before-after examples using safe generic or mock examples.
- Trust section based on team marketplace experience, without making Ken the public influencer face.
- FAQ that reinforces scope boundaries.

### B. Paid Audit Funnel

Use paid audits as the main trust-building front door.

Flow:
1. Prospect buys or requests a $97-$197 audit.
2. Team reviews listings and delivers a concise findings report.
3. Report includes a recommended next step: Cleanup, Monthly Management, or Performance Trial.
4. If they do not continue, the audit still generates revenue and proof of demand.

Why this works:
- Avoids free custom consulting.
- Filters for prospects willing to pay.
- Creates a natural upsell without cold follow-up pressure.
- Keeps the first delivery scoped and delegatable.

### C. Warm Relationship Conversations

Allowed route: Ken or the brand can discuss the new offer with people who already know him/the business, when the conversation is natural and not a cold sequence.

Soft script:
"We are repositioning MyCommercePartner around Amazon/eBay marketplace cleanup and management. The first front-door offer is a paid listing audit where we identify the biggest issues in a seller's listings or store, then recommend whether cleanup or monthly management makes sense. If you know a seller whose marketplace presence is messy but has potential, that is the kind of client we can help."

No pressure, no automated follow-ups, no cold prospecting.

### D. Referral System

Create simple referral partners:
- Web designers who build ecommerce sites but do not manage Amazon/eBay.
- Local marketing agencies without marketplace specialists.
- Bookkeepers/accountants serving product businesses.
- Product photographers and packaging designers.
- Small business consultants.
- Fulfillment/3PL contacts.
- Shopify/WooCommerce freelancers whose clients also sell on marketplaces.

Referral offer:
- 10% of first paid project or first month management fee.
- Or reciprocal referral where appropriate.
- Keep it simple and documented.

Partner positioning:
"We help sellers clean up and manage their Amazon/eBay presence after the product, website, photography, or fulfillment foundation is in place. We do not replace your service; we help your clients perform better on marketplaces."

### E. Content-Led Inbound

Publish practical content that attracts sellers already searching for help:
- "Why eBay listings get views but no sales."
- "Amazon listing cleanup checklist for small brands."
- "How to tell if your marketplace store needs cleanup."
- "Amazon vs eBay listing mistakes that hurt buyer trust."
- "When to hire marketplace management instead of doing it yourself."
- "What a marketplace listing audit includes."
- "How missing item specifics hurt eBay sales."
- "Simple ways product brands can improve marketplace conversion."

CTA on every piece:
- "Start with a Marketplace Listing Audit."

### F. Public Proof Without Making Ken The Face

Use brand/team-led trust assets:
- "Our team has helped marketplace operations improve listings, store processes, and sales performance across Amazon/eBay environments."
- Generic before/after examples.
- Anonymous case-style stories.
- Process diagrams.
- Checklists.
- Screenshots/mockups that do not expose employer/client data.

Avoid:
- Making Ken the influencer face.
- Naming sensitive past employers or internal details.
- Mentioning internal tooling/AI/agents publicly.

### G. Light Paid Acquisition To Paid Audit Page

Only after the offer page is clear:
- Run small-budget search/social tests pointing to the paid audit page.
- Target seller pain, not broad business owners.
- Budget cap: small test budget only until conversion data exists.
- Success metric: paid audit purchases or qualified review requests, not vanity traffic.

Suggested initial ad angles:

## Revenue Strategy Notes

### Primary Revenue Sequence

Audit ($97-$197) → Cleanup ($297-$997) → Monthly Management ($500-$2,000+/month)

### Small Seller Sequence

Audit or direct review → 60-day Performance Trial ($200-$300/month + optional upside) → Starter Management ($500/month)

### Pricing Discipline

- Do not discount by default.
- Use smaller scope instead of lower price.
- Use paid audit to avoid free consulting.
- Use performance trial only when baseline and upside are measurable.
- Any custom project above Store Rescue should become a clearly scoped statement of work, not open-ended service.

### Delegation Discipline

Every offer should be executable by a trained worker using:
- Intake checklist.
- Audit template.
- Cleanup checklist.
- Monthly reporting template.
- Escalation rules for risk/account decisions.

Ken should only approve pricing exceptions, risk decisions, major positioning, or client exceptions.
