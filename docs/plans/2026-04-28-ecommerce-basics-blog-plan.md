# Ecommerce Basics Blog Plan — 2026-04-28

## Goal
Add an educational blog section to MyCommercePartner that teaches product brands the basics of Amazon, eBay, listing quality, buyer trust, and marketplace cleanup.

## Positioning
The blog should support the parent brand identity: hands-on Amazon/eBay account management for product brands. It should build trust by teaching practical basics in plain English.

## Tone rules
- Beginner-friendly and useful.
- Plain English; avoid jargon unless explained.
- Customer-facing copy only.
- Do not mention internal tools, AI, bots, agents, or automation.
- Tie lessons back to listings, marketplace content, account health, and weekly improvement.

## Initial structure
- `blog.html` — blog hub / Ecommerce Basics index
- `blog-amazon-listing-basics.html` — beginner Amazon listing guide
- `blog-ebay-title-basics.html` — beginner eBay title guide
- `blog-marketplace-review-basics.html` — simple marketplace review checklist

## Conversion path
Every article should end with a soft CTA to the free listing audit or Listing Rescue. The blog should educate first, sell second.

## Acceptance criteria
- Blog is linked from the main navigation.
- Blog hub has at least three starter articles.
- Articles teach practical basics and do not overpromise results.
- Public pages avoid internal tooling language.
- All links resolve locally before deployment.
