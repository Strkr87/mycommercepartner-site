## Lead qualification checklist
Mark a lead qualified if 4+ are true:
- Existing live site on Wix, Shopify, or similar platform
- Sells products, quote-led products, wholesale, industrial, or ecommerce
- Site looks outdated, passive, confusing, or weak on mobile
- One obvious page or path is underperforming
- Business likely benefits from more quotes, inquiries, or trust
- Decision-maker or operator is reachable directly
- Can likely pay $750–$1,500 without enterprise procurement friction
- Problem can be fixed without a full rebuild first

## Daily outreach operating target
- 15 targeted first messages per day
- 5 follow-ups per day
- Goal: 2 qualified reply threads by day 3
- Goal: 1 payment-link or direct Basic checkout push by day 5

## Prospect research note
Every outbound message should mention one specific weakness from the target site:
- weak headline clarity
- buried CTA
- confusing quote/contact step
- missing trust/proof near the top
- cluttered mobile scan path

Never send a generic intro without one concrete observation first.
