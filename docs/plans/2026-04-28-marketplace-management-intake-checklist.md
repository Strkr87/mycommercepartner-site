## Scope Fit

- Not a fit

## Red Flags

Do not proceed without Ken approval if any are true:

- Seller wants guaranteed sales.
- Seller asks for unpaid exact fixes.
- Seller wants daily customer support included.
- Seller wants marketplace appeal/suspension help.
- Seller has risky/restricted/counterfeit/gray-market products.
- Seller refuses to share basic baseline data.
- Seller wants unlimited custom work for a low package price.
