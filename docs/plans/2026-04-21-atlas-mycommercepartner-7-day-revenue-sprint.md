# Atlas — MyCommercePartner 7-Day Revenue Sprint

**Start date:** 2026-04-21
**Sprint goal:** Generate the first incoming revenue for MyCommercePartner within 7 days.
**Owner:** Atlas (Portfolio Operator / General Manager)
**Escalation:** Theo

---

## Primary objective
Get paid client activity within 7 days by pushing the fastest-to-close offer first, tightening the site around that offer, and creating a simple outbound + follow-up operating rhythm.

## Revenue priorities (in order)
1. **Basic Upgrade ($750)** — easiest fast-close service offer
2. **Growth Upgrade ($1,500)** — main target offer
3. **Monthly support ($150/mo)** — only after initial project interest

## Success definition
At least one of the following happens inside 7 days:
- one paid Basic or Growth purchase
- one signed client agreement with payment scheduled
- one highly qualified sales conversation with near-term close probability and a next-step payment date

---

## Atlas operating rules
- Focus on **revenue and lead generation**, not broad brand-building.
- Prefer actions that can create client conversations **this week**.
- Keep the offer simple: website conversion upgrades for outdated Wix/Shopify/product-business sites.
- Favor direct response over passive marketing.
- If a site change is obviously needed to improve conversion, make it directly in the repo and push it.
- Maintain a short running ops log and KPI tracker in:
  - `/home/ken_m/mycommercepartner-site-check/docs/plans/atlas-mcp-ops-log.md`

---

## Daily KPI targets
Track and update each run:
- outbound messages sent
- qualified replies received
- calls/conversations booked
- proposal/payment links sent
- paid conversions
- blockers

---

## Day 1 priorities
1. Confirm live site and offer ladder are consistent.
2. Create or refine the shortest outbound pitch for the Basic and Growth packages.
3. Create a short lead qualification checklist.
4. Produce a simple lead tracker if one does not exist.
5. Identify the single biggest bottleneck to revenue this week.

## Day 2–7 priorities
On each run:
1. Review live site and sales assets.
2. Update the ops log with KPI counts and current bottleneck.
3. Take the highest-leverage next action from this list:
   - improve homepage CTA or proof
   - tighten package framing
   - draft outbound messages
   - create follow-up templates
   - create lead tracker / proposal template / payment flow copy
   - prepare case-study style before/after material
   - define a daily outreach quota
4. Report back with:
   - what changed
   - current KPI counts
   - next 1–3 actions
   - whether we are on track for revenue inside 7 days

## Messaging angle
Use this positioning:
- done-for-you conversion upgrades
- best for outdated sites that feel weak, confusing, or passive
- works especially well for Wix, Shopify, wholesalers, industrial suppliers, and quote-led product businesses
- fixed-scope packages and clear next steps

## Default outreach angle
- short audit/review offer
- point out one obvious homepage or conversion weakness
- offer a simple fixed-scope first step
- move quickly toward a paid Basic Upgrade when appropriate
