## 3. Simple FAQ Objections And Answers

### How do I know which offer is right for me?
Start with the Marketplace Listing Audit if you are unsure. It gives you a clear view of what is hurting your listings and which next step makes sense.

### Do you guarantee more sales?
No. Marketplace sales depend on many things, including price, demand, competition, inventory, shipping, reviews, images, and account health. We focus on improving the parts we can directly help with: listing quality, buyer clarity, store presentation, and marketplace organization.

### Can you just tell me what to fix for free?
We can give a high-level overview before you buy, but exact recommendations and fixes are part of the paid audit or cleanup package. That keeps the work fair, useful, and properly scoped.

### Do you handle buyer messages and customer service?
No. Our standard packages do not include daily buyer messages, returns, refunds, or customer service. We focus on marketplace listing and store improvement.

### Do you manage inventory or fulfillment?
No. Inventory, fulfillment, shipping operations, and stock decisions stay with your business unless a separate scope is agreed in writing.

### Can you fix a suspended account or appeal a marketplace decision?
Suspensions, appeals, restricted products, and high-risk account issues require separate review. We do not accept that work inside a standard audit, cleanup, management plan, or small-seller trial.

### Do I need to give you account access?
For an audit, listing URLs and screenshots may be enough. For cleanup or management, we may need limited access or a clear approval process. We will only ask for the access needed to complete the agreed work.

### How long does an audit take?
Most small audits can be completed within 2-3 business days after we receive the required listing links, screenshots, and intake details.

### How long does a cleanup take?
Timing depends on the package size. Smaller cleanups may take 3-5 business days. Larger cleanup projects may take 7-10 business days after intake, access, and approvals are ready.

### What if my store has more listings than the package includes?
We will focus on the highest-priority listings first. If the store needs more work, we can recommend a larger cleanup, monthly management, or a clearly scoped next project.

### Why should I pay monthly instead of doing it myself?
Many sellers know their listings need work but never have time to keep improving them. Monthly management gives you a practical marketplace support team without hiring a full-time specialist.

### What makes a seller a good fit?
Good-fit sellers have real products, active Amazon or eBay listings, clear ownership of the products, enough margin to support the work, and a willingness to keep the scope clear.

### What makes a seller a bad fit?
We are not a fit for sellers who want unpaid custom work, guaranteed sales, daily support, unclear product sourcing, high-risk categories without documentation, or unlimited requests inside a small package.

## 4. NCSNA Pricing Script And Softer Fallback

### Primary NCSNA-Style Script

"Based on where the store is now, I would not recommend jumping straight into a large monthly contract. A cleaner first step is a 60-day marketplace improvement trial.

We would set a simple baseline at $1,500 in monthly sales, clean up the priority listings, improve the store presentation, and measure whether the changes create enough lift to justify continuing.

The trial would be $200 per month plus 15% of monthly sales above $1,500. So if the store does not grow past that baseline, the cost stays low. If it does grow, we share in the upside.

After 60 days, we can either move into a simple monthly plan, adjust the scope, or stop with no long-term contract."

### If They Object To 15%

"That is fair. If 15% feels too high, we can soften the structure and do $250 per month plus 10% of monthly sales above the $1,500 baseline.

That keeps the upfront cost reasonable while still making sure the work is tied to results. The scope would stay focused on priority listing cleanup, store presentation, and measurable marketplace improvements during the 60-day trial."

### If They Want No Percentage

"We can also keep it flat and simple: $300 per month for 60 days, with no performance share.

That would cover a limited improvement cycle, but it would also mean the scope stays tighter. At the end of 60 days, we review the account and decide whether it makes sense to continue into monthly management."

### Boundary Reminder

"To keep the trial affordable, it would not include daily customer service, fulfillment, inventory management, marketplace appeals, or unlimited edits. The focus is listing quality, store cleanup, buyer trust, and priority marketplace improvements."

### Close

"If that works, the next step is to confirm the current sales baseline, choose the priority listings, collect the needed links or access, and start the first cleanup cycle."
