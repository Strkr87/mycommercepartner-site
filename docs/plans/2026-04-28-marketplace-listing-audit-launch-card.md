Lead with the **$197 Standard Audit** when the seller has an active store or several listings.

- the seller is skeptical
- they only have a few listings
- they need a low-risk first step

## After payment workflow

1. Confirm payment notification in Stripe/email.
2. Ask for marketplace links if not already submitted.
3. Create an internal audit file using:
   - `docs/plans/2026-04-28-marketplace-listing-audit-template.md`
4. Review the requested Amazon/eBay pages.
5. Deliver a high-level audit with priority fixes.
6. Offer one next step only:
   - Store Cleanup Package
   - Monthly Marketplace Management
   - Performance-Based Trial
   - or no further action if the account is not a fit

## Fulfillment promise

Keep the paid audit practical and high-level.

Do include:
- what is hurting buyer clarity
- what looks incomplete or weak
- what should be fixed first
- which paid next step fits best

Do not include:
- a full done-for-you rewrite
- exact implementation for every fix
- a large free consulting report
- technical backend details
