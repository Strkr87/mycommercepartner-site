# MyCommercePartner Pivot — Brand-Led Marketplace Management Company

Date: 2026-04-28
Owner: Ken / Strkr
Command center: Theo

## Decision

MyCommercePartner is pivoting into a brand-led marketplace management company focused on Amazon and eBay support for product brands, online sellers, and small business owners.

The public business should not depend on Ken being the face of the company right now. The brand/team should be the face. Ken stays behind the scenes as strategist/operator while Theo coordinates workers and specialists.

## Positioning

Plain-English positioning:

> MyCommercePartner helps product brands and online sellers clean up, manage, and grow their Amazon and eBay marketplace presence without needing to hire a full-time marketplace team.

Shorter version:

> A practical marketplace management team for Amazon and eBay sellers.

## Fit With Ken

This direction fits Ken because:

- He prefers hands-off delegation over doing every task himself.
- He likes managing people and building systems.
- He is strongest at ideas, strategy, and problem-solving.
- He has proven wins building teams and hitting record Amazon/eBay numbers.
- He can sell, but does not want the business to depend on cold calling.
- He does not want heavy customer support.
- He wants brand/team-led public positioning because he is currently full-time employed.

## First Four Offers

### 1. Marketplace Listing Audit

Purpose: low-friction entry offer.

What it includes:
- Review a seller's Amazon/eBay listings or storefront.
- Identify obvious title, description, image, pricing, trust, and conversion issues.
- Deliver a short, high-level recommendation summary.
- Keep exact fixes and implementation details paid.

Suggested pricing:
- Free or low-cost lead magnet for very small prospects.
- $97–$197 paid audit for more serious prospects.

### 2. Store Cleanup Package

Purpose: simple fixed-scope paid offer.

What it includes:
- Clean up a limited number of listings.
- Improve listing titles, item specifics, descriptions, basic store presentation, and buyer trust cues.
- Give the seller a simple next-step plan.

Suggested pricing:
- $297 basic cleanup.
- $497 deeper cleanup.
- $997 larger store rescue.

### 3. Monthly Marketplace Management

Purpose: recurring revenue.

What it includes:
- Ongoing listing improvement.
- Store/account cleanup.
- Basic performance review.
- Marketplace task coordination.
- Monthly improvement report.

Suggested pricing:
- Starter: $500/month.
- Growth: $1,000/month.
- Managed: $2,000+/month.
- Optional performance bonus on growth above agreed baseline.

### 4. Performance-Based Trial For Small Sellers

Purpose: close smaller sellers like NCSNA without pricing them out.

Default offer:

> $200/month + 15% of monthly sales above $1,500, 60-day trial, no long-term contract.

Fallback if they resist 15%:

> $250/month + 10% of monthly sales above $1,500.

Simple flat option:

> $300/month flat for 60 days.

## What Not To Build

Avoid business models that require:

- Ken doing customer support all day.
- Ken being the personal public influencer/founder face right now.
- Cold calling constantly.
- Custom work with unclear scope.
- Low-margin tasks that cannot be delegated.
- Unbounded client requests without a clear package boundary.

## Sales Motion

Preferred acquisition should be brand-led and trust-led:

- Website pages that explain each offer clearly.
- Educational blog/resources around Amazon/eBay basics.
- Warm referrals and direct relationship opportunities.
- Small paid audits and cleanup packages.
- Before/after style examples using safe generic/mock examples.
- Partnerships with ecommerce service providers.

Current cold outreach freeze remains in effect. Do not send cold emails, follow-ups, apology emails, or prospecting emails unless Ken explicitly approves a new compliant route.
