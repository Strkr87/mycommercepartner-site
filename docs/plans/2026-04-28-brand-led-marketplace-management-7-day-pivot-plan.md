# 7-Day Pivot Plan — Brand-Led Marketplace Management

Date: 2026-04-28
Business: MyCommercePartner
Direction: Brand-led Amazon/eBay marketplace management company

## Goal

Move MyCommercePartner from a scattered/mixed offer into a clear, brand-led marketplace management company with four simple entry offers:

1. Marketplace Listing Audit
2. Store Cleanup Package
3. Monthly Marketplace Management
4. Performance-Based Trial for small sellers like NCSNA

## Ground Rules

- Ken stays behind the brand/team for now.
- Theo is command center.
- Workers execute specialized tasks and report in their channels.
- No cold outreach or follow-up outreach during the current freeze unless Ken explicitly approves a new compliant route.
- Public website copy must not mention internal AI/tooling/agent language.
- Offers must be simple, scoped, and delegatable.

## Day 1 — Lock Offer Ladder And Positioning

Owner: Stark with Theo coordination

Deliverables:
- Offer ladder: audit → cleanup → monthly management → performance trial.
- Simple package/pricing table.
- Qualification rules: who is a fit / not a fit.
- NCSNA-style small seller trial language.
- Short pitch script that does not rely on cold calling.

Acceptance criteria:
- Ken can explain the business in one sentence.
- Each offer has scope, price range, outcome, and next step.

## Day 2 — Website Structure Update

Owner: Vera

Deliverables:
- Homepage reflects brand-led marketplace management.
- Clear four-offer path on site.
- Existing pages updated or reorganized around the new direction.
- CTA paths point to contact/request review.

Acceptance criteria:
- Visitor understands within 10 seconds: Amazon/eBay marketplace support.
- No page feels like a random unrelated offer.
- Public-copy forbidden-term scan passes.

## Day 3 — Offer Copy And Sales Assets

Owner: Clara

Deliverables:
- Plain-English copy for each of the four offers.
- One short proposal/one-pager outline.
- FAQ objections and simple answers.
- NCSNA pricing script.

Acceptance criteria:
- Copy is simple enough to send to a prospect without rewriting.
- No jargon-heavy or internal/tooling language.

## Day 4 — Delivery SOPs

Owner: Theo coordinating Stark/Clara/Vera as needed

Deliverables:
- Audit SOP.
- Store Cleanup SOP.
- Monthly Management SOP.
- Performance Trial SOP.
- Client intake checklist.

Acceptance criteria:
- A worker or contractor could execute the first version without Ken doing every step.

## Day 5 — Proof And Trust Assets

Owner: Clara + Vera

Deliverables:
- Safe generic before/after examples.
- Short case-style story based on Ken's proven capability, without naming his employer or exposing sensitive details.
- Website trust section language.
- Proposal PDF outline.

Acceptance criteria:
- Builds credibility without Ken becoming public face.
- Uses generic/mock examples only unless permission exists.

## Day 6 — Inbound/Referral Acquisition Plan

Owner: Stark

Deliverables:
- No-cold-call lead sources.
- Partnership/referral ideas.
- Content topics that attract online sellers.
- Warm conversation scripts.
- Paid audit funnel plan.

Acceptance criteria:
- No cold outreach required.
- Plan can generate early conversations from warm channels, content, and referrals.

## Day 7 — Review And Launch Readiness

Owner: Theo + Stark

Deliverables:
- What is live.
- What is missing.
- Next 14-day priority list.
- Go/no-go checklist for first paid client.

Acceptance criteria:
- MyCommercePartner has a clear public direction.
- First paid offer can be pitched safely.
- Ken only needs to approve blockers and key positioning.
