# Marketplace Management SOPs — First Version

Date: 2026-04-28
Business: MyCommercePartner
Purpose: Make the four new offers repeatable, scoped, and delegatable.

## Operating Rules

- Ken stays behind the brand/team.
- Theo coordinates work and delegates execution.
- No daily customer support, buyer messages, returns, fulfillment, or inventory management in standard offers.
- Exact fixes stay inside paid work.
- Any account-risk, suspension, restricted-product, legal/compliance, or pricing exception escalates to Ken before acceptance.

---

## SOP 1 — Marketplace Listing Audit

### Goal
Give the seller a high-level, practical review of what is hurting their Amazon/eBay listings and recommend the right next paid step.

### Intake Needed
- Seller name and business name.
- Marketplace: Amazon, eBay, or both.
- Store/listing URLs.
- Product category.
- Number of listings to review.
- Current pain: visibility, conversion, store cleanup, account organization, or unclear.
- Current monthly sales range if available.
- Any restricted/compliance-sensitive product notes.

### Scope
- Intro audit: up to 5 listings.
- Standard audit: up to 10 listings.
- Review titles, item specifics/bullets, descriptions, images, pricing signals, shipping/trust cues, and buyer confidence.
- Deliver high-level priorities only.

### Not Included
- Rewriting listings.
- Making account changes.
- Buyer messages/customer support.
- Inventory/fulfillment.
- Advertising management.
- Marketplace appeals or restricted-product risk work.

### Delivery Steps
1. Confirm payment/intake is complete.
2. Save listing URLs and screenshots if needed.
3. Review each listing using the audit template.
4. Identify the top visibility, conversion, trust, and completeness issues.
5. Choose recommended next offer: Cleanup, Management, Trial, or Bad Fit.
6. Deliver a concise findings report.

### Escalate To Ken If
- Seller asks for guaranteed sales.
- Product/category looks restricted or risky.
- Client wants account access before scope is clear.
- Seller asks for free exact fixes.

## SOP 2 — Store Cleanup Package

### Goal
Improve a fixed number of listings/store details so the seller's marketplace presence looks clearer, more complete, and easier to trust.

### Packages
- Basic Cleanup — $297 — up to 5 listings.
- Standard Cleanup — $497 — up to 10 listings.
- Store Rescue — $997 — up to 25 listings or one focused category.

### Intake Needed
- Paid package selection.
- Listing URLs/account access or approved edit workflow.
- Product details/spec sheets if available.
- Brand restrictions and compliance notes.
- Approval contact.
- Current listing screenshots before work.

### Delivery Steps
1. Confirm scope, payment, and access.
2. Create before-work record/screenshots.
3. Prioritize listings by revenue potential, traffic, or obvious issues.
4. Improve titles, key details, description structure, item specifics/bullets, and buyer clarity.
5. Flag anything risky instead of changing it blindly.
6. Create after-work summary.
7. Recommend next step: monthly management, another cleanup block, or stop.

### Not Included
- Daily management.
- Customer support.
- Product photography.
- Legal/compliance claims.
- Advertising spend management.
- Unlimited revisions.

### Escalate To Ken If
- Any claim could create compliance risk.
- Client requests work outside package limits.
- Account access feels unsafe.
- Category/product looks restricted.

## SOP 3 — Monthly Marketplace Management

### Goal
Provide recurring marketplace improvement support without becoming daily customer support or operations staff.

### Tiers
- Starter — $500/month — up to 10 listings monitored/improved monthly.
- Growth — $1,000/month — up to 30 listings monitored/improved monthly.
- Managed — $2,000+/month — larger catalog or deeper coordination.

### Monthly Cycle
1. Month kickoff: confirm priority listings and account goals.
2. Week 1: review performance/listing issues and choose priorities.
3. Week 2: implement approved listing/store improvements.
4. Week 3: review store/category presentation and remaining issues.
5. Week 4: deliver monthly summary and next-month recommendations.

### Standard Report
- What was reviewed.
- What was improved.
- What still needs attention.
- Any risks/blockers.
- Next month priorities.

### Communication Boundaries
- Async scheduled communication only.
- No daily buyer support.
- No emergency account handling unless separately approved.

## SOP 4 — Performance-Based Trial

### Goal
Give small sellers a lower-risk 60-day improvement test without turning into unlimited low-cost work.

### Default Terms
- $200/month + 15% of monthly sales above $1,500 baseline.
- 60-day trial.
- Fallback: $250/month + 10% above $1,500.
- Flat option: $300/month for 60 days with tighter scope.

### Intake Needed
- Current 30/60/90-day sales baseline.
- Listing/store URLs.
- Priority products.
- Agreement on what counts as marketplace sales.
- Agreement on scope and reporting.

### Delivery Steps
1. Confirm baseline and payment.
2. Pick priority listings.
3. Perform first cleanup cycle within 7 business days.
4. Review at day 30.
5. Review at day 60.
6. Recommend continue, upgrade, adjust, or stop.

### Escalate To Ken If
- Seller disputes baseline.
- Seller wants unlimited edits or support.
- Seller has no reliable sales data.
- Performance-share terms are unclear.
